Today's research workflow

  1. Find a real business and verify it exists.

Quality standard

Never invent phone numbers, email addresses, decision makers or business information. If a fact cannot be confirmed, leave it blank and document the source.

Duplicates, incomplete contact data and uncertain facts should be flagged for review rather than marked Sales Ready.
