Quality standard

Import Review

Common column names are automatically mapped (Business Name, Contact, Phone, Email, Website, Industry, Address, Notes, Source). The CSV Source column becomes research evidence, not the CRM import channel. Long sources are preserved in Notes when space permits; otherwise the preview explains what to edit. Without a header, CSV columns use that order. One-name-per-line lists are also supported. Existing CRM duplicates are skipped by the server.

Important: Imported names without phone, email or website can be saved but still need research. SBC Lead Assist automatically checks saved leads; Lorna does not manually approve them.
