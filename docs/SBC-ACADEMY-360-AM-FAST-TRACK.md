# SBC Academy 360 · Experienced Account Manager Fast Track (V66.165)

**Owner decision:** SBC hires experienced account managers, not novices who need a week of generic sales education. Standard onboarding target is **two authorized paid training shifts (up to eight hours of SBC official training-clock time)**. Successful assessment is competency based, so experienced employees may demonstrate knowledge without sitting through arbitrary video-watching time. **No automatic extension, overtime authorization, pay denial, termination or access grant.**

## Competency and budget contract

- Pre-hire screen (before paid orientation): demonstrate prior account ownership, lead-to-renewal outcomes, demo experience, structured CRM notes, bilingual conversation ability, ethical objections; practice scenarios use fiction.
- **First shift (up to 4h):** SBC standards, Fantastic 4, Account 360, research vs outreach permission, needs discovery, local business website concept.
- **Second shift (up to 4h):** approved pricing/quotes, Square capture, owner approval, objections, durable handoff, retention and final observed owner capstone.
- Exactly two normally authorized shift entries and 480 minutes from the EXISTING SBC official training time clock. Academy never alters, recalculates, deletes or caps actual payable working time. The time clock/payroll are separate.
- At the budget: stop further Academy score/role-play/completion writes; display OWNER_REVIEW_REQUIRED. Keep completed records viewable. **Do not auto-clock-out, stop pay, assign mandatory unpaid homework or auto-terminate.** The owner reviews performance and decides what is next.
- One optional owner extension is limited to 240 additional training minutes and one further shift, and requires explicit authorization and a 20–500 character reason.
- Live account API access stays blocked until all eight stages are completed, each of twelve objective checks passes at 90%+, each module has a documented practice record, an owner passes the final observed capstone, and the owner records current Certified status for this Academy course. Generic training endpoints must not bypass the gate.
- Academy does not issue calls, texts, purchases, vendor orders, compensation, refunds or lead assignments.

## Eight certification stages / twelve labs

| Stage | Labs | Practical proof |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Prospect intelligence | SBC identity, Fantastic 4, Account 360 | Sourced fictional account and accurate CRM notes |
| 2. Website-first discovery | Research trust and consultative discovery | Permission-safe opening; verified vs unverified facts |
| 3. Visual demo | Website Studio truthful preview | Placeholder disclosure and actual scope |
| 4. Consultative proposal | Published tiers and objections | Approved-scope comparison |
| 5. Square and receipt | Quote and payment safeguards | Recognize capture vs pending/refund |
| 6. Owner governance | Follow-up and escalation | Owner approval boundaries |
| 7. Durable handoff | Support and renewal | Detailed customer care plan |
| 8. Capstone | End-to-end simulation | Owner-observed role-play and safe handoff |

## Media truthfulness

Academy includes original illustrated CSS motion walkthroughs, storyboard scenes, visibly labeled fictional UI, built-in browser English and Spanish read-aloud, adjustable voice speed, pause/restart, and interactive server-graded assessments. The motion player is a **training illustration, not a real video recording** of SBC software or customer conversations. No bogus video player or stock testimonials. **Real screen-capture videos and owner voice recordings are not provided in this release**; obtain them later from owner-approved non-customer fixtures, and add captions.

## Accountability and access

All access requires authentication, matching tenant, writer lease for writes and recovery readiness. The answer key is server-only. Legacy training attempts, progress and certification routes are guarded. Browser content can never self-certify or grant a live-call permission. There is no automatic actual $1 Square test or charge.

## Payroll and legal review

Required job-related employee training normally counts as compensable time under 29 CFR 785.27 except when all four criteria for exclusion apply. Florida wage law is $15/hour from September 30, 2026 for covered Florida employees. Remote international workers may have different wage/classification requirements according to actual work location and employer relationship; get appropriate advice before engagement. The training budget is NEVER a ceiling on legally owed wages.
