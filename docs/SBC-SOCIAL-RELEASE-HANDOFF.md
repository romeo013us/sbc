# SBC marketing public-asset handoff candidate

Offline-only function buildSocialRelease in social-release.cjs re-renders each approved marketing PNG through the existing social-jpeg staging engine with the pinned original logo, requires the trusted operator's final JPEG SHA-256 and current review version, and packages at most three content-addressed JPEGs with a manifest for the SBC website. The private receipts are separate from the ZIP. It rejects exact source reuse against the supplied authoritative history and exact output duplicates in the batch. Exact hash checks are not perceptual similarity detection; the caller must provide complete history and check visual uniqueness. The synthetic test logo is not an approved business logo.

The caller must be a trusted offline operator, not a request handler, and confirm actual final bytes, rights, content, intended public scope and approved logo hash. Upload, public HTTP readback, verification of deployed bytes, revocation/withdrawal, publisher manifest integration and social publication are not implemented by this module. Keep the candidate ZIP private until final review and current approval recheck; do not expose customer media. Public URLs in the manifest are prospective destinations, not proof of availability. A returned candidate is never a published social post. No paid services or new routes.
