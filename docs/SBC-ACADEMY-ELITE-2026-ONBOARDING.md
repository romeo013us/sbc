# SBC Academy — 2026 Elite Fantastic 4 Training (V66.107)

**Decision:** 12 guided hours for each new Lead Researcher or Sales Caller. The Academy replaces obsolete published V15/V17/Big 3 training for active employees with two current six-hour courses: shared Foundations + role-specific Mastery.

This is an instructional plan, not a guaranteed competence claim, substitute for supervised onboarding, or an automatic payroll calculation. The owner must approve readiness. The existing browser voice/read-aloud remains unchanged.

## Workable schedules

| Option | Day 1 | Day 2 | Day 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Two 6-hour shifts** | Shared Foundations modules 1–6 (360 minutes) | Role Mastery modules 1–6 (360 minutes) | Not needed |
| **Three 4-hour shifts** | Shared modules 1–4 (240 minutes) | Shared modules 5–6 + Role modules 1–2 (240 minutes) | Role modules 3–6 (240 minutes) |

Each 60-minute module budgets **15 minutes** for reading/listening to the lesson, **30 minutes** for role-play or safe scenario practice, and **15 minutes** for self-check, evidence and feedback. Adjust pacing for actual comprehension. Existing official time clock determines hours worked, not Academy estimated time; applicable paid breaks/labor requirements remain under business policy.

## Pass / fail and safety rules

- **Every module:** actual readable lesson and practice exercise, documented evidence/reflection plus learner acknowledgement. Lesson completion is not owner certification.
- **Written assessment:** two case prompts per lesson and an **85% target** for owner/manager-reviewed assessments. Existing V2 Training Center tracks attempts/remediation; the current UI may not collect formal written assessment automatically.
- **Live skills:** owner observes researcher source/provenance, correct duplicates and caller opening/discovery/objections/CRM reporting. Customer data, real charges and vendor actions MUST NOT be used in simulations.
- **Critical stop conditions:** role crossover, privacy breach, opting out ignored, unapproved claims, payment status guessed, fabricated leads or unauthorized financial action. Failure requires remediation; owner controls certification, clearance and real outreach.
- **Ongoing coaching:** 12h is the initial bootcamp, not the entire ramp period. Observe and coach first real shifts; measure accuracy and consent, then contact rates and meaningful handoffs.

## Data migration and rollback

- New 2026.11 course and module IDs prevent old lesson progress being falsely counted as completion.
- Six built-in legacy courses become archived; their modules, assignments, attempts, reviews, progress, certifications and events are preserved.
- A versioned idempotent installer runs during training state initialization, with a persistence marker surfaced through catalog repair.
- Lorna access repair points to current courses, not legacy built-ins; employee course views omit archived assignments.
- No role, certification, owner UI, Twilio, database schema, payment, website, employee layout or read-aloud changes.
