# SBC Revenue First — Caller and Closing Playbook (human-initiated only)

Updated 2026-11-09. This playbook supports current CRM and sales workspace. It does not authorize bulk calling, SMS campaigns, automated dialing, advertising or spend.

## Daily execution
1. Use Revenue Sprint owner brief to review up to 20 *verified, callable* leads. Confirm the correct business number and legal contact eligibility, DNC and local calling rules before making any attempt.
2. Call personally or through the existing human-initiated Twilio dialer. Do not represent research-complete as consent.
3. Log actual results: connected, voicemail, not interested, needs discovery, wants a quote, follow-up time, or do-not-contact.
4. Identify ONE actual pain point. Recommend only the package that meets it. Verify price and fulfillment cost in the active product catalog before quoting.
5. If requested, send a written quote and the approved Square checkout link, then record the next agreed follow-up. Never claim payment until provider evidence reconciles.
6. Reconcile paid orders and vendor costs; fulfilled status alone is not cash.

## Simple discovery opener (not an automated recording)
"Hi, this is [caller] with Successful By Choice in Miami. Did I reach the person who handles how customers find [business]? I'll be brief. When someone searches for your business today, do they find accurate information and an easy way to contact you?"

Pause. Listen. Ask permission before continuing. Respect a no or removal request immediately.

### If visibility is the main need
"We offer a business-visibility setup that can help organize your Google Business Profile information and provide printed business cards. Our current entry package is listed at $99, subject to your business needs and the current catalog. Would you like a quick breakdown of what's included?"

### If they genuinely need a website
"We also offer a $199 Business Starter option with a one-page website, first-year domain/hosting and business cards, subject to the package's active terms. First I'd want to check what you already have so we don't sell you something unnecessary."

### If they already have a site
"That's good—I'm not going to sell you another website. What happens after someone lands there? Is it easy for them to call, request service, and find accurate hours and directions?" Evaluate a specific maintenance/SEO service only if supported by a real finding.

## Objections
- "We already have someone." -> "Understood. We can leave your current provider alone. Would a no-pressure check of your listing accuracy or printed materials be useful?"
- "Too expensive." -> "What budget would make sense? I can compare only the services you actually need without adding unnecessary extras."
- "Send info." -> "Certainly. Which issue should I focus on, and what email may I use? When would you prefer I follow up?"
- "Not interested." -> "Understood. I'll mark that in our records so you aren't contacted again." Record suppression when requested.

## Owner's daily scoreboard
- 20 human-reviewed attempts is a planning target, not proof a person answered.
- Track verified contacts, actual conversations, quote requests, quotes sent, paid customer orders and follow-ups due.
- Never label a Square $1 internal QA checkout as a customer sale.
- Enter actual supplier/product/labor costs before calling an order profitable.
- Do not launch paid ads on an unproven offer. No new SaaS tool is required for this playbook.

## First 30-day go/no-go
- Start with zero new advertising spend. Sell to verified CRM leads, existing personal connections, referrals and local walk-ins.
- After at least three genuine paid customers and a confirmed positive contribution per sale, consider one small, owner-approved local paid test within $75 monthly cap.
- Review weekly: contact-to-conversation, conversation-to-quote, quote-to-payment, contribution profit and follow-up completion.
- Stop channels that consume more money than the measured contribution they produce.

The owner's target is sustainable customer revenue and positive cash flow, not an arbitrary number of calls or promises of 3-4 sales/day.
