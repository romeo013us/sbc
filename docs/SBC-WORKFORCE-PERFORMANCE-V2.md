# SUPER SBC Workforce Performance V2
Status: first increment, advisory only; not a deployed or activated compensation policy.
## Owner-approved operating policy
Standard: $5/hour, 30 hours/week, $500 weekly collected revenue, 9% eligible gross profit commission; English required, Spanish preferred. Senior: $6/hour, 30 hours/week, $1,000 weekly collected revenue, 12% eligible gross profit commission; English and Spanish required and live-verified. Monthly commission eligibility requires signed terms. Biweekly base pay; payment lag and classification require compliance review before activation. Earned pay is never withheld for missing sales targets.
30-calendar-day trial; Academy 360 90% per stage, eight paid training hours; first sale by workday 5, two distinct new paying customers by workday 10, major review by workday 15. Quotas and early milestones are separate. All personnel decisions require owner review and consideration of company-side barriers.
## Owner-approved competitive trial (two candidates, one current opening)
- Recruit and interview multiple applicants. Enroll up to two qualified candidates in a fully paid 30-calendar-day trial only after both accept written terms and funds are reserved for all earned compensation. One long-term opening currently exists; a second may open if demand, contribution and cash reserves support it. Neither candidate is promised retention.
- Communicate this arrangement before acceptance; training, hourly pay and earned compensation are not contingent on winning or meeting quota. Confirm lawful worker classification, payment timing, and applicable termination requirements before enrollment.
- Compare candidates over the same selling period with equivalent lead quality and opportunity volume, language/market fit, product access, coaching and functioning tools. Track differences and adjust interpretations, not earned pay. Standard and Senior retain their own quotas and language requirements.
- Owner review rubric (proposed weights): 40% verified net collected sales versus signed quota; 20% contribution after attributable direct costs; 15% distinct new paying customers; 10% documented call quality; 10% follow-up/account management; 5% training/reliability. Publish criteria in advance. Missing or disputed evidence is not a zero; flag it for review. Do not generate an automatic hiring, termination, or ranking decision from this rubric.
- Review workdays 5, 10 and 15 and calendar day 30; consider lead availability, training, outages, refunds, compliance, quality, and actual profit. The owner may retain both, one, or neither, subject to contract and law. A comparative result never overrides minimum role requirements or profitability.
- Initial two-Standard base reserve: $1,200 for four weeks, excluding commissions and other costs; Standard plus Senior: $1,320. Reserve for the entire agreed trial and any additional obligations before starting. Do not rely on future customer payments.
- UI acceptance: private individual progress, cohort-level targets, no identifiable peer compensation or customer records in worker view. In a two-person cohort, anonymous peer ranks can still reveal the other person's results; default to personal progress and cohort milestones, with full comparison available only to the owner.
- Candidate notice: "SBC currently has one long-term Account Manager opening and may create a second if business performance supports expansion. Both selected candidates will receive paid training and agreed hourly compensation throughout their trial. We will assess results and account-management quality fairly; both candidates may be retained if they meet our standards and the business can support both. Earned compensation is paid regardless of the retention decision."
- Implementation acceptance: effective-dated cohort membership, signed notice acknowledgment, tenant-scoped review evidence, lead distribution audit, payroll reserve check, owner-only comparative review, and auditable owner decisions. No automatic personnel actions or production activation until verified. This section is a specification, not an implemented cohort workflow.
