Publish criteria in advance. Missing or disputed evidence is not a zero; flag it for review. Do not generate an automatic hiring, termination, or ranking decision from this rubric.

The owner may retain both, one, or neither, subject to contract and law. A comparative result never overrides minimum role requirements or profitability.

- UI acceptance: private individual progress, cohort-level targets, no identifiable peer compensation or customer records in worker view. In a two-person cohort, anonymous peer ranks can still reveal the other person's results; default to personal progress and cohort milestones, with full comparison available only to the owner.
- Candidate notice: "SBC intends to hire two Standard Account Managers, subject to qualification, funding and individual paid-trial review. Both selected candidates will receive paid training and agreed hourly compensation throughout their trial. We will assess results and account-management quality fairly; both may continue if they individually meet our standards and the business can support both. Earned compensation is paid regardless of the retention decision."
- Implementation acceptance: effective-dated cohort membership, signed notice acknowledgment, tenant-scoped review evidence, lead distribution audit, payroll reserve check, owner-only comparative review, and auditable owner decisions. No automatic personnel actions or production activation until verified. This section is a specification, not an implemented cohort workflow.
