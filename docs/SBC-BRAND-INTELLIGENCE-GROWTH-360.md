# SBC Brand Intelligence & Growth 360 — first delivery

## Research basis and independent implementation
- Pentagram: https://www.pentagram.com/about/ and https://www.pentagram.com/work/mastercard — practitioner-led identity work and recognition-preserving visual systems.
- Landor: https://landor.com/en/our-work/saudia-airlines/ and https://landor.com/our-work/tataev/ — unified portfolio, physical and digital experience.
- Wolff Olins: https://wolffolins.com/work/decathlon — coherent architecture, voice, motion and employee alignment.
- COLLINS: https://wearecollins.com/case-studies/naturalizer/ and https://wearecollins.com/case-studies/youtube-gaming/ — audience insight translated into experience and positioning.
- Interbrand: https://interbrand.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/IB-Brand-Valuation_220608.pdf — documented brand-strength and financial valuation framework. SBC's rubric is independent, not a proprietary Interbrand model or financial valuation.

## Implemented
Brand evidence rubric (ten dimensions, explicit unknowns, dated source and observation; full score requires full coverage); tenant-bound draft brand contract with deterministic content hash and contrast-checked CSS tokens; date-scoped campaign presets in the existing private owner marketing workspace. The November 26 question is hypothetical, not a giveaway. November 27 campaigns do not promise unapproved discounts. All drafts require original logo and media review, and do not publish or spend. No new routes. The rubric does not independently fetch or validate the truth of evidence; the contract does not verify logo bytes or grant brand approval. It is not yet persisted or wired into site/print rendering. Private creative workspace rendering, review and JPEG staging remain separate.
