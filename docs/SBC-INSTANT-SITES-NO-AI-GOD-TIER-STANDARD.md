# SBC Instant Sites — God Tier, No-AI-Required Product Standard

**Owner direction (November 13, 2026):** Build an SBC-native website-production and customer-success system aiming to outperform instant website builders on the quality of the **final customer website**, without depending on per-site generative AI tokens or another paid builder SaaS. Preserve existing Command Center, Account 360, Office Box, and production safeguards.

## One connected customer workflow

Research and verify → build tailored private concept → owner/client review → quote and payment authorization → gather approved assets and copy → publish with authorized hosting/domain/SSL → verify live functions and metrics → ongoing named Account Manager.

An instant **private concept** can be generated in seconds. A **public customer site** must not be called complete until domain, verified content, SSL, forms and QA actually pass. DNS propagation, customer approval, and hosting provider provisioning cannot be promised in seconds.

## Deterministic design without AI credits

1. Use the existing SBC site factory, experience module and visual engine as the native generator.
2. Offer distinct industry archetypes and stable per-business visual directions. A refresh must not randomly change the customer brand; no paid AI endpoint is needed.
3. Pull verified business identity, researched opportunities and location from tenant-scoped CRM records. Unverified data remains visibly unverified. Never fabricate testimonials, awards, prices, credentials, photos, staff or addresses.
4. Keep the customer lifecycle in one authoritative Account 360 record: lead research, assigned manager, quote, site concept, fulfillment and support.
5. Preserve customer authorization, tenant isolation, DNC/SMS consent, payments, owner authority, recovery locks and the single MySQL writer.
6. AI may be an **optional future assistant**; every customer project must be completable without AI or surprise recurring API charges.

## Site quality: evidence, not a fictional score

The existing generator is a **concept engine**, not yet a fully operational customer-domain publisher. Owner approval for a prospect presentation is NOT customer website launch authorization. Its demonstration forms intentionally do not collect visitor data.

Acceptance criteria for public delivery:

| Dimension | Evidence required |
|---|---|
| Business content | Customer-approved name, services, contact methods, location/service area and accurate claims |
| Brand and media | Approved brand direction, cleared image rights and optimized images; no misleading imagery |
| Accessibility | Keyboard support, visible focus, labels, adequate contrast and assistive-tech review |
| Mobile performance | Lighthouse and actual deployed-site loading/Web Vitals measurements |
| SEO | Accurate metadata, canonicals, sitemap, indexing, internal links and truthful schema markup |
| Forms/booking | End-to-end tested delivery, privacy and anti-spam safeguards, working confirmed CTAs |
| Hosting and SSL | Domain authorization, DNS and HTTPS verification, redirects, rollbacks and uptime plan |
| Payment and approval | Durable payment/contract evidence, final customer approval and vendor/margin checks |
| Account success | Assigned manager, support channel, agreed scope, follow-ups and change history |

**9.5/10 is a target, never an auto-awarded grade.** Initial quantitative acceptance targets for actual deployed sites: mobile Lighthouse Performance >=90, Accessibility >=95, Best Practices >=95, SEO >=95, and zero critical functional/security failures. Scores must be evidence-backed; these are NOT claims of achieved results.

## Packages and permissions

- $199 Starter: one-page site. Confirm entitlement and margins for any promised GoDaddy Websites + Marketing plan; SBC custom HTML is NOT automatically an editable Airo/Website Builder template.
- $349 Basic: four-page static-capable output after final assets, contact integration and the agreed hosting configuration.
- Lorna/research: record verifiable phone, source, business address where publicly available, and research notes; mobile/service-area-only businesses must not have invented street addresses.
- Account Manager: nurture assigned contacts, follow up, prepare presentations, coordinate service and escalate purchases/support. The existing technical caller role retains least-privilege server gates until a separately reviewed account manager RBAC expansion.
- Owner/fulfillment: control payment confirmation, hosting purchases, domain/DNS changes, publication, vendor spending and sensitive CRM edits.

## Roadmap and explicit release boundary

Phase 1 (this PR): CRM-generated Instant Sites brief; stable no-AI business-specific visual palettes; Account Success plan; Lorna address notice; honest launch-quality gate. No new SaaS, AI calls or automatic publication.

Phase 2: versioned content and media editing; client-reviewed copy and responsive page templates; owner-approved imagery and differentiated industry composition.

Phase 3: verified static export, real inquiry/booking integrations, sitemap and canonical URLs, authorized domain/SSL automation, rollback, secure credentials and cost approvals.

Phase 4: live Lighthouse/accessibility/SEO audits, verified release score, fulfillment receipts, customer support/renewals and fair competitor comparisons using equal measurement methods.

**Do not publish prematurely:** Require passing V66 reproducible ZIP, security, tenant, and production build checks; perform controlled Preview smoke tests; verify production address schema before using new editing functionality. Never bypass identity checks, consent, payment evidence, DNS permissions or customer approval.
