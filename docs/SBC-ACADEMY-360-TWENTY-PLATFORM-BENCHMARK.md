Reviewed November 22, 2026. This is a practical comparison of twenty established learning and enablement systems, not a universal worldwide ranking. The SBC features below are original implementations of useful learning patterns. They do not integrate, license or reproduce these vendors' proprietary course libraries.

## Experienced-hire operating plan

The normal assignment is two scheduled paid training shifts, with 480 minutes of official training-clock time. Core lesson allocations total 400 minutes; 80 minutes remain for review and flexibility. Allocation is a planning target, not a watch-time requirement. An experienced hire may demonstrate competence sooner.

| Stage | Modules | Core allocation |
|---|---|---|
| Prospect intelligence | am-01, am-02, am-03 | 60 minutes |
| Discovery and contact boundaries | am-04, am-05 | 60 minutes |
| Honest concept demonstration | am-07 | 35 minutes |
| Scope, proposal and objections | am-06, am-08 | 65 minutes |
| Square and verified payment | am-10 | 30 minutes |
| Follow-up and owner controls | am-09 | 25 minutes |
| Delivery, retention and renewals | am-11 | 35 minutes |
| Bilingual capstone | am-12 | 90 minutes |
| Review and flexibility | Across both shifts | 80 minutes |

Each module requires a knowledge pass, structured sample pass, an original fictional practice note and recorded completion. The three-question knowledge check requires all three correct to meet the 90% minimum. Critical errors fail practical work regardless of the numerical result. Lessons am-08 and am-12 additionally require the branching conversation to pass.

The final owner rubric covers discovery/listening, scope accuracy, demonstration, objections, controls, handoff and bilingual communication. Each dimension is 0–4; passing requires at least 90%, every dimension at least 3, no critical violation, documented observation in English and Spanish, a real observation reference, and specific notes. A written practice note does not prove spoken fluency.

At 480 minutes or two completed training shifts, assessment writes stop pending an owner decision. One explicit exception may add at most 240 minutes and one shift, with a reason. No budget action edits clock entries, stops pay, grants customer access or makes an employment decision.

## What remains before a production-quality claim

- Hosted Preview acceptance using test employees, including database restart and backup restoration with the real MySQL persistence path.
- Visual review on actual desktop, tablet and mobile browsers. The local preview browser could not reach the execution environment; automated DOM integration is not a substitute for rendered layout acceptance.
- Owner-approved SBC screen recordings, captioned videos and native protected recording uploads. The included player is an illustrated workflow with browser speech, not a recording of the live application. Narration availability depends on the user's browser and installed voices.
- Optional spaced refreshers and collaborative authoring improvements, with explicit training-time authorization.

No platform endorsement, guaranteed sales improvement, universal top-twenty ranking or 9.5 production score is claimed.
