# SBC V61.0.0 — EXECUTIVE SOVEREIGNTY

SBC Executive Overmind is the coordinating executive decision layer above the State Kernel, Commercial OS, Money Intelligence and Evolution Engine.

## Core behavior
- Maintains a standing economic objective: maximize sustainable collected gross profit.
- Evaluates business context and ranks economic plays.
- Can explicitly DISAGREE with an owner proposal when policy/economic evidence indicates a better alternative.
- Produces rationale, objections, expected value, confidence, authority level and ranked alternatives.
- Uses delegated authority: autonomous, bounded, or owner-signature.
- Preserves absolute owner veto.
- Blocks autonomous high-risk financial, credential, legal, destructive-data and security-boundary actions.
- Records executive decisions for audit and learning.
- Recovery-safe mode prevents mutation while authoritative CRM recovery is incomplete.

## Executive endpoints
- GET /api/executive-overmind/status
- GET /api/executive-overmind/mandate
- POST /api/executive-overmind/challenge
- GET /api/executive-overmind/decisions

This release does not claim unrestricted self-modification. Production changes remain governed, reversible, auditable and subject to the delegated authority matrix.
